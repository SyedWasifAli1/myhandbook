# Wasif style cooking recipe

* [Aloo Gosht](aloo-gosht.md) - A comforting Wasif Style Aloo Gosht featuring tender mutton pieces and soft potatoes simmered in a spiced onion-tomato gravy.
* [Beef Pulao](beef-pulao.md) - A traditional Wasif Style Beef Pulao featuring tender bone-in beef chunks, rich meat broth (yakhni), and fragrant whole spices.
* [Chicken Biryani](chicken-biryani.md) - A signature Wasif Style aromatic chicken biryani recipe with layered basmati rice, tender spiced chicken, and caramelized onions.
* [Chicken Karahi](chicken-karahi.md) - A fast, robust Wasif Style Chicken Karahi cooked with fresh tomatoes, ginger juliennes, green chilies, and cracked black pepper.
* [Chicken Qorma](chicken-qorma.md) - A royal Wasif Style Chicken Qorma with a velvety yogurt-onion gravy, fragrant kewra water, and warm traditional spices.
