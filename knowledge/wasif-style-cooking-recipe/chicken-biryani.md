## Overview

This guide details the authentic preparation of Wasif Style Chicken Biryani, combining fragrant long-grain basmati rice, deeply spiced chicken masala, and rich saffron-infused milk.

## Recipe Details

- **Recipe Name:** Chicken Biryani
- **Preparation Time:** 45 minutes
- **Cooking Time:** 50 minutes
- **Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken Marinade & Curry:
- Chicken (cut into pieces): 1 kg
- Yogurt (whisked): 1 cup
- Ginger garlic paste: 2 tablespoons
- Red chili powder: 1.5 teaspoons
- Turmeric powder: 0.5 teaspoon
- Coriander powder: 1 tablespoon
- Biryani masala powder: 2 tablespoons
- Tomatoes (chopped): 3 medium
- Green chilies (slit): 4-5
- Fresh mint leaves: 1/2 cup
- Fresh coriander leaves: 1/2 cup
- Cooking oil or ghee: 1/2 cup
- Onions (thinly sliced): 3 large (for brown onions / baresta)

### For the Rice:
- Long-grain Basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes): 3 cups
- Water: 4-5 liters (for boiling)
- Whole spices (Bay leaves 2, Green cardamom 4, Cloves 4, Cinnamon stick 1, Black peppercorns 6, Star anise 1): Whole spice mix
- Salt: 3 tablespoons (or to taste)
- Lemon juice: 1 tablespoon
- Oil/Ghee: 1 tablespoon

### For Layering & Garnishing:
- Saffron strands: A generous pinch
- Warm milk: 1/4 cup
- Yellow or orange food color: A few drops (optional)
- Ghee: 3 tablespoons
- Mint and coriander leaves (chopped): Handful

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Prepare Baresta (Crispy Brown Onions):** Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pot and fry the sliced onions on medium-high heat until deeply golden brown and crisp. Remove half the onions for final garnishing and leave the rest in the pot.
2. **Marinate the Chicken:** In a bowl, combine chicken pieces, yogurt, ginger garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, biryani masala, half of the mint/coriander leaves, and half of the fried onions. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes.
3. **Cook the Chicken Curry:** Add the marinated chicken to the pot with remaining oil/onions. Cook on medium-high heat for 10 minutes, then add chopped tomatoes. Cover and simmer until the chicken is tender and oil separates from the masala (approx. 20 minutes).
4. **Boil the Rice:** In a large separate pot, bring water to a rolling boil with salt, whole spices, lemon juice, and 1 tablespoon of oil. Add soaked basmati rice and cook until it is 70-80% cooked (firm to the bite). Drain completely.
5. **Layering (Dum):** In a heavy-bottomed heavy pot (or the same curry pot), layer half of the partially cooked rice over the chicken gravy. Sprinkle chopped mint, coriander, and remaining fried onions. Add the remaining rice on top.
6. **Saffron & Ghee Infusion:** Dissolve saffron strands in warm milk and drizzle over the rice along with melted ghee and food color if desired.
7. **Dum Cooking:** Seal the pot tightly with a tight-fitting lid or aluminum foil. Cook on high heat for 5 minutes, then reduce heat to the lowest setting (dum) and cook for 20-25 minutes. Turn off heat and let it rest for 10 minutes before opening.
8. **Serving:** Gently fluff the biryani from the side to mix the white and yellow rice with the spiced chicken underneath. Serve hot with raita and salad.

## Cooking Tips

- **Rice Soak:** Soaking aged basmati rice for 30 minutes ensures extra-long, unbroken grains after boiling.
- **Spice Level:** Adjust green chilies and red chili powder according to personal preference.
- **Resting Period:** Never skip the 10-minute resting period after turning off the stove; it allows the steam to distribute evenly and prevents the rice from breaking.
