## Overview

This guide details the preparation of Wasif Style Aloo Gosht, a soul-satisfying traditional curry featuring succulent mutton chunks and potatoes cooked in a rich, spiced gravy.

## Recipe Details

- **Recipe Name:** Aloo Gosht
- **Preparation Time:** 20 minutes
- **Cooking Time:** 50 minutes
- **Servings:** 5-6 servings

## Ingredients

- Mutton or Lamb (bone-in pieces): 1 kg
- Potatoes (peeled and halved or quartered): 4 large
- Cooking oil or Ghee: 1/2 cup
- Onions (finely sliced): 2 medium
- Tomatoes (pureed or finely chopped): 2 medium
- Ginger-garlic paste: 2 tablespoons
- Yogurt (whisked): 1/2 cup
- Red chili powder: 1.5 teaspoons
- Turmeric powder: 1/2 teaspoon
- Coriander powder: 1.5 teaspoons
- Salt: 1.5 teaspoons
- Whole spices (Bay leaf 1, Green cardamom 3, Black cardamom 1, Cloves 4, Cinnamon stick 1): Whole spice mix
- Garam masala powder: 1 teaspoon
- Fresh coriander leaves (chopped): 1/4 cup
- Water: 3-4 cups (for gravy)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sauté Onions & Whole Spices:** Heat oil or ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot or pressure cooker. Add whole spices and sliced onions. Fry until onions turn golden brown.
2. **Brown Mutton:** Add ginger-garlic paste and mutton pieces. Sauté over medium-high heat for 8-10 minutes until the mutton is well browned and aromatic.
3. **Add Spices & Tomatoes:** Add red chili powder, turmeric powder, coriander powder, and salt. Stir for 2 minutes, then add chopped or pureed tomatoes along with whisked yogurt. Cook until oil separates from the masala.
4. **Pressure Cook or Simmer:** Add 2 cups of water. Cover and pressure cook for 20-25 minutes (or simmer covered on medium-low heat for 45-50 minutes) until the mutton is nearly tender.
5. **Add Potatoes:** Add the potato halves into the pot along with another cup of water for gravy consistency. Simmer uncovered for 12-15 minutes until potatoes are fork-tender and the mutton is fully cooked.
6. **Finish with Garam Masala:** Sprinkle garam masala powder and fresh chopped coriander leaves over the curry. Cover and let rest on low heat for 5 minutes.
7. **Serving:** Serve hot with soft tandoori rotis, boiled white rice, or parathas.

## Cooking Tips

- **Potato Cut:** Cutting potatoes in halves prevents them from dissolving during the simmering process.
- **Gravy Adjustments:** Adjust water quantity at the potato stage depending on whether you prefer a thicker or soupier shorba (gravy).
