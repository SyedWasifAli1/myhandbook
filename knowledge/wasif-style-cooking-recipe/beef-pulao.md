## Overview

This guide details the preparation of Wasif Style Beef Pulao, renowned for its deeply flavorful meat broth (yakhni) and perfectly separated grains infused with warm whole spices.

## Recipe Details

- **Recipe Name:** Beef Pulao
- **Preparation Time:** 30 minutes
- **Cooking Time:** 1 hour 15 minutes
- **Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

### For the Yakhni (Broth):
- Beef (bone-in chunks): 1 kg
- Water: 7 cups
- Fennel seeds (saunf): 2 tablespoons
- Coriander seeds (sabut dhania): 2 tablespoons
- Onion (quartered): 1 large
- Garlic cloves: 8-10 whole
- Ginger (sliced): 1 inch piece
- Salt: 1.5 tablespoons

### For the Pulao Masala & Rice:
- Long-grain Basmati rice (soaked for 30 minutes): 3 cups
- Ghee or Cooking oil: 1/2 cup
- Onions (sliced): 2 medium
- Ginger-garlic paste: 1 tablespoon
- Yogurt: 1/2 cup
- Whole spices (Bay leaves 2, Green cardamom 5, Black cardamom 2, Cloves 6, Cinnamon sticks 2, Black peppercorns 10, Cumin seeds 1 tablespoon): Whole spice mix
- Green chilies (slit): 4-5
- Garam masala powder: 1 teaspoon
- Fresh mint leaves: 1/4 cup
- Salt: To taste (adjust after yakhni reduction)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Prepare the Yakhni (Broth):** In a large pot or pressure cooker, combine beef, water, fennel seeds, coriander seeds, quartered onion, garlic cloves, ginger slices, and salt. Cook until the beef is tender and melt-in-the-mouth (about 40-50 minutes in a pressure cooker or 1.5 hours in a standard pot). 
2. **Strain Broth:** Strain the yakhni, collecting the rich broth in a container (measure to ensure about 5 cups of liquid; add water if needed) and set the tender beef chunks aside. Discard the spent aromatics.
3. **Brown the Onions:** In a large heavy-bottomed pot, heat ghee or oil and fry the sliced onions until golden brown. Remove a small portion for final garnish.
4. **Sauté Beef & Spices:** Add whole spices and ginger-garlic paste to the remaining onions. Fry for 1 minute, then add the boiled beef chunks. Sauté well for 5 minutes.
5. **Add Yogurt & Aromatics:** Whisk in the yogurt along with slit green chilies and mint leaves. Cook until the oil separates from the yogurt.
6. **Add Yakhni Broth:** Pour in the measured yakhni broth. Bring to a rolling boil.
7. **Cook Rice:** Add the soaked and drained basmati rice. Stir gently. Cook on medium-high heat until most of the broth is absorbed and little tunnels (craters) form on the rice surface.
8. **Dum Cooking:** Sprinkle garam masala powder and reserved browned onions on top. Cover tightly with a foil-sealed lid. Simmer on the lowest heat setting (dum) for 20 minutes.
9. **Serving:** Fluff the pulao gently before serving. Serve hot with mint raita and kachumber salad.

## Cooking Tips

- **Broth Quality:** The secret to incredible pulao lies in simmering the yakhni long enough for the bones to release their natural gelatin and richness into the broth.
- **Water Ratio:** Always measure the strained yakhni accurately against the rice volume (typically 1.5 to 1.75 cups of liquid per 1 cup of aged basmati rice).
