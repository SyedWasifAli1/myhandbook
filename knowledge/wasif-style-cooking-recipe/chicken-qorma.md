## Overview

This guide details the preparation of Wasif Style Chicken Qorma, featuring a rich, silken yogurt gravy thickened with fried onions and infused with subtle cardamom and kewra water.

## Recipe Details

- **Recipe Name:** Chicken Qorma
- **Preparation Time:** 25 minutes
- **Cooking Time:** 35 minutes
- **Servings:** 4-5 servings

## Ingredients

- Chicken (cut into pieces): 1 kg
- Ghee or Cooking oil: 1/2 cup
- Onions (thinly sliced): 3 medium
- Yogurt (whisked smooth): 1.5 cups
- Ginger-garlic paste: 2 tablespoons
- Coriander powder: 2 tablespoons
- Red chili powder: 1 teaspoon (or to taste)
- Kashmiri red chili powder: 1 teaspoon (for rich color)
- Salt: 1.5 teaspoons
- Green cardamoms: 4-5 whole
- Cloves: 4 whole
- Cinnamon stick: 1 medium
- Nutmeg and mace powder (jaifal & jawatri): 1/4 teaspoon
- Kewra water: 1 teaspoon
- Blanched almonds or cashews (optional paste): 2 tablespoons (for extra richness)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Fry the Onions (Baresta):** Heat ghee or oil in a deep pot. Fry the sliced onions until crisp and golden brown. Remove them with a slotted spoon, spread them on a paper towel to cool, and then crush or coarsely blend them once cooled.
2. **Sear Chicken & Aromatics:** In the remaining warm ghee, toss in green cardamoms, cloves, and cinnamon. Add chicken and ginger-garlic paste. Sauté on medium-high heat for 6-8 minutes until chicken turns white and opaque.
3. **Add Spices & Yogurt:** Lower the heat. Add coriander powder, red chili powder, Kashmiri chili powder, and salt. Stir well for 2 minutes. Gradually pour in the whisked yogurt while stirring continuously to prevent curdling.
4. **Simmer the Gravy:** Cover and cook on medium-low heat for 15 minutes until the chicken is tender and the oil floats to the surface.
5. **Add Crushed Onions:** Stir in the crushed fried onions (and optional nut paste if using). Simmer for another 5 minutes on low heat, allowing the gravy to thicken into a rich, velvety consistency.
6. **Finishing Touch:** Sprinkle nutmeg and mace powder along with kewra water over the qorma. Cover and let it rest on low heat (dum) for 5 minutes.
7. **Serving:** Serve hot with freshly baked sheer mal, taftan, or garlic naan.

## Cooking Tips

- **Yogurt Technique:** Always beat the yogurt smooth and lower the flame or turn off the heat temporarily when adding it to the pot to prevent splitting.
- **Onion Texture:** Crushing the fried onions by hand or with a mortar and pestle gives a more authentic texture than a smooth blender puree.
