## Overview

This guide details the preparation of Wasif Style Chicken Karahi, an iconic wok-based curry celebrating robust tomato flavors, fresh ginger, and minimal dry spices.

## Recipe Details

- **Recipe Name:** Chicken Karahi
- **Preparation Time:** 15 minutes
- **Cooking Time:** 25 minutes
- **Servings:** 4 servings

## Ingredients

- Chicken (cut into curry pieces): 800g
- Tomatoes (ripe, halved lengthwise): 5-6 medium
- Ginger-garlic paste: 1.5 tablespoons
- Green chilies (slit): 4-6
- Fresh ginger (cut into fine juliennes): 2 tablespoons
- Red chili flakes: 1 teaspoon
- Black peppercorns (freshly cracked): 1 teaspoon
- Cumin powder (freshly roasted): 1 teaspoon
- Coriander powder: 1 teaspoon
- Salt: 1 teaspoon (or to taste)
- Cooking oil or Ghee: 1/2 cup
- Fresh coriander leaves (chopped): 3 tablespoons

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Sear the Chicken:** Heat oil or ghee in a traditional iron or heavy-bottomed karahi (wok) over high heat. Add the chicken and ginger-garlic paste. Fry for 5-7 minutes until the chicken changes color and turns lightly golden.
2. **Add Tomatoes:** Lower the heat slightly and arrange the halved tomatoes cut-side down over the chicken. Cover with a lid and let cook on medium heat for 8-10 minutes until the tomato skins soften.
3. **Remove Skins & Mash:** Remove the lid, use tongs to easily peel away the softened tomato skins, and discard them. Mash the remaining tomato pulp into the chicken using a wooden spoon or spatula.
4. **Build Flavors & Bhunai (Roasting):** Turn the heat back to high. Add salt, red chili flakes, roasted cumin powder, and coriander powder. Stir vigorously (bhunai) for 5-8 minutes until the oil separates clearly from the masala and coats the chicken.
5. **Finish with Aromatics:** Toss in half of the ginger juliennes and slit green chilies. Stir well for 1 minute.
6. **Final Garnish:** Sprinkle freshly cracked black pepper and remaining ginger juliennes and fresh coriander leaves on top.
7. **Serving:** Serve piping hot directly in the karahi alongside freshly baked naan or tandoori roti.

## Cooking Tips

- **High Heat Control:** Karahi relies heavily on high-heat stir-frying (bhunai) at the end to get that signature restaurant-style texture.
- **Fresh Pepper:** Always use freshly cracked black pepper at the very end rather than pre-ground powder for authentic aroma and punch.
- **No Added Water:** The moisture from the chicken and the natural juices of the tomatoes are entirely sufficient for cooking. Avoid adding water.
