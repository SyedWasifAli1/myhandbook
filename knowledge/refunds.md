Customers can return an item within 30 days when they have a receipt.[^refund-policy-interview-2026-09-13]

Returned items must be unused. Refunds are issued within 5 working days.[^refund-policy-interview-2026-09-13]

Faulty goods are exempt from the 30-day return window.[^refund-policy-interview-2026-09-13]
